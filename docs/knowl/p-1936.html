Andaikan \(m^2<2\) dan tetapkan \(\delta=2-m^2>0\text{.}\) Sifat Archimedes memberi bilangan bulat positif \(n\) dengan \(n>(2m+1)/\delta\text{.}\) Karena \(n\geq1\text{,}\) diperoleh
\begin{equation*} \left(m+\frac1n\right)^2 =m^2+\frac{2m}{n}+\frac1{n^2} \leq m^2+\frac{2m+1}{n} <m^2+\delta=2. \end{equation*}
