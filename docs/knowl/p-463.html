Misalkan \(n\) bilangan bulat positif dan \(x = (x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n)\text{,}\) \(y=(y_1, y_2, \ldots, y_n)\) berada di dalam \(\R^n\text{.}\) Maka
\begin{equation*} \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i+y_i)^2} \leq \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n x_i^2} + \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n y_i^2}\text{.} \end{equation*}
